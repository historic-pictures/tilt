- `models/horse.glb` — "Horse" by Quaternius (quaternius.com), via poly.pizza/m/qvTrSG9pZF, CC0.

## Photo-scanned models — Poly Haven, CC0

`scan/<name>/` holds the glTF as downloaded (1K textures) plus the leaf cut-out map.

- Trees: `jacaranda_tree`, `island_tree_01`, `island_tree_02`, `tree_small_02`. The game does not load these (1–4 million triangles each);
  it loads `impostor/<name>_col.jpg / _nor.png / _occ.png / .json`, sixteen views of each tree baked by `tools/bake.html`.
  The four tree folders (about 410 MB) are only needed to re-bake and can be deleted.
- Placed as they are: `rock_moss_set_01`, `wooden_barrels_01`, `wine_barrel_01`, `wooden_crate_02`, `wooden_bucket_01`, `tree_stump_01`.
- Downloaded but not used yet: `boulder_01`, `kite_shield`, `wooden_lantern_01`, `modular_fort_01`, `large_castle_door`, `fern_02`, `shrub_02/03/04`.

## Fonts — SIL Open Font License 1.1

`fonts/` (latin subset, woff2, as served by Google Fonts; the licence texts are alongside as `OFL-*.txt` and must ship with the game).

- **Grenze Gotisch** (Bold) — Omnibus-Type. A gothic textura: titles, rubrics, scores and callouts.
- **EB Garamond** (Medium, regular and italic) — Georg Duffner and Octavio Pardo. All reading text; the "EB Garamond SC" face is the same font with the lowercase mapped to its small capitals (labels, menu entries and buttons).
- Source: github.com/google/fonts (ofl/grenzegotisch, ofl/ebgaramond), subset to Latin and converted to woff2.

## Heraldic charges (`assets/heraldry/charges*.webp`)

The beasts, plants, objects and crosses in the charge atlas are drawings from Wikimedia Commons, all public domain or CC0: the engravings of A. C. Fox-Davies, *A Complete Guide to Heraldry* (1909) as vectorised on Commons, lions by Inductiveload, the heraldic crosses by AnonMoos and others. One entry per file (page, author, licence) is in `tools/heraldry/licences.json`; `tools/heraldry/build_atlas.py` turns them into the atlas. No attribution is owed; it is recorded all the same.

## Notification lettering (`assets/fonts/PirataOne-400.woff2`, `GWLombardic.woff2`)

The callouts, judges' and scorer's calls, toasts, lesson headings, card titles and the celebration prompt are set as a red Lombardic initial followed by Pirata One in mixed case (src/lombard.js, ui.css "notifications"). Both are Latin subsets in woff2 (Lombardic A-Z only; it has no digits or punctuation).
- Pirata One, by Rodrigo Fuenzalida and Nicolas Massi, SIL Open Font License 1.1 (`assets/fonts/OFL-pirataone.txt`).
- Lombardic, by George Williams (1988-2001), BSD 3-clause licence: the copyright notice and disclaimer are in `assets/fonts/BSD-gwlombardic.txt`. The author's name is not used to endorse the game.
