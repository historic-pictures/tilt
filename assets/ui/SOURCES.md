## Icons (game-icons.net, CC BY 3.0)

The gold-leaf icons (helm, horse head, laurels and trophy, cog, mounted knight, castle, open book, coins) are from https://game-icons.net, repository https://github.com/game-icons/icons, licensed Creative Commons Attribution 3.0 (https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/3.0/), commercial use allowed with attribution:
- "Visored helm", "Horse head", "Cog", "Open book" by Lorc
- "Laurels trophy", "Castle", "Coins" by Delapouite
- "Mounted knight" by Skoll

They are embedded as a small inline sprite in `src/icons.js` (backgrounds removed, filled with a gold gradient and given an ink outline). Attribution: icons by Lorc, Delapouite, Skoll and contributors, CC BY 3.0, game-icons.net.

## Painted icons, wordmark and app icon (AI-generated for TILT)

`icons/*.webp` (armoury, stable, renown, settings, career, joust, learn, coins, spur, brace, shield at 256, 128 and 64 px; `wordmark.webp`, `wordmark-320.webp`) and `../app/*.png` (favicon, 192, 512, maskable 512, apple-touch-icon 180) were generated for TILT with the owner's own AI image pipeline (1024 px originals in `shots/genart/icons/`), then cut out onto transparency, trimmed, centred and resampled by script. Generated by the owner for TILT, so no third-party licence applies. The game-icons.net glyphs in `src/icons.js` remain as the fallback if a picture fails to load.

## Logo (AI-generated for the game, then drawn by hand)

`icons/emblem-{256,128,64}.webp` and the app icons in `../app/` (icon-192, icon-512, icon-maskable-512 on lapis, apple-touch-icon on parchment, favicon-32) come from the owner's AI-generated emblem (the knight in a gold-rimmed lapis roundel, lance breaking out of the rim; cut-outs in `shots/genart/logos/`). `logo.svg` is a simplified vector redraw made by hand in SVG (flat fills, ink outlines, readable at 16-64 px) for the favicon and print.
