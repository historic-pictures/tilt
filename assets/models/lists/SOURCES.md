# The lists: sources

`lists_lod0.glb`, `lists_lod1.glb`, `lists_lod2.glb` are our own work, generated by `tools/blender/lists/build_lists.py` (headless Blender 5.2: numpy geometry, vertex occlusion by ray casting, our own glb writer). They carry geometry only; the timber and linen textures are the scans the game already ships (`assets/tex`, see `assets/CREDITS.md`), the cloths and arms are drawn at run time (`src/lists.js`).

## Reference images

None were downloaded. The brief allowed up to 25 from Wikimedia Commons; the Commons API answered HTTP 429 to an anonymous client on every attempt in this session, so `shots/lists/ref/` is empty and no licence entries are needed. The shapes were built from the works below as known, not traced over images; if references are fetched later, list each file here with its licence.

## Works the shapes follow

| Work | What was taken from it |
|---|---|
| King Rene of Anjou, *Traite de la forme et devis d'un tournoi* (BnF fr. 2695, c. 1460) | The lists as a post-and-rail fence with gates at the ends; the timber galleries (berfrois) on posts with boarded, hung fronts and a breast-high parapet; the judges' own stand |
| Westminster Tournament Roll (College of Arms, 1511) | The plank tilt under a cloth; the royal gallery with canopy, valance, cloth of estate and arms; banded posts with pennons |
| Froissart, *Chroniques* (BL Harley 4379, c. 1470-72), the jousts of Saint-Inglevert | Striped awnings with scalloped valances, the stand behind a hung parapet, lance racks |
| Jean de Wavrin, *Chroniques d'Angleterre* (BL Royal 14 E IV, c. 1470-80) | Stand and barrier carpentry, the heralds' and trumpeters' low stage |
| *Freydal* (KHM Vienna, c. 1512-15) | The tilt's boards, posts and rail; painted poles |
| Reconstructed lists: Royal Armouries tiltyard (Leeds), Hever Castle | Tilt height and section, fence and gate carpentry, the mounting block |
